Tracejar 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026-07-14；更新 / Updated：2026-10-26（私密相册独立密码与生物识别开关 / separate Private Album security）

中文

1. 一句话总结

Tracejar 没有服务器。 你的记录只存在你的设备上，以及（当你开启 iCloud 同步时）你自己的私人 iCloud 里。开发者无法访问你的任何内容。我们不收集、不分析、不出售你的数据，也没有任何广告或第三方 SDK。

2. 我们是谁

Tracejar 由 Tongwei Zhang 独立开发。联系方式：zhangtongwei@gmail.com

3. 我们不收集什么

  • ❌ 不收集手机号、邮箱、通讯录、账号（App 无需注册登录）
  • ❌ 不收集设备标识符、广告标识符（IDFA）
  • ❌ 无任何第三方 SDK、无分析、无崩溃统计、无广告
  • ❌ 不做任何跨 App/网站的跟踪

App 内置的隐私清单（Privacy Manifest）中，「收集的数据类型」为空。

4. 你的数据存在哪里

你的日记内容（文字、照片、视频、语音、心情、标签、可选的位置）存储在：

1. 你的设备本地（通过 Apple 的 SwiftData）； 2. 你自己的私人 iCloud 数据库——仅当你在设置中开启「iCloud 同步」（默认开启）。这属于你的 Apple ID 私有区域，开发者没有任何访问权限。

若你使用中国大陆 Apple ID，iCloud 数据由云上贵州（GCBD）运营；使用其他区域 Apple ID 时，由 Apple Inc. 在相应司法辖区存储。我们不持有任何服务器端数据。

6. 应用锁、伪装模式与私密相册（可选）

  • App 锁密码从不以明文存储或以明文离开设备。每台设备在 Keychain 里存「随机盐 + SHA-256 哈希」；同 Apple ID 的设备会经 iCloud 钥匙串同步一个只包含随机校验标记的加密信封，用来验证同一个 App 锁密码。它不包含日记内容或照片钥匙。
  • 私密相册使用独立密码。你放进去的照片会先在设备上加密（AES-GCM，CryptoKit），加密用的随机主钥匙再由相册密码推导出的钥匙锁住（PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256，60 万轮）。只有被锁住的主钥匙信封会进入私人 iCloud 数据库和 iCloud 钥匙串；App 锁密码不能打开相册，相册密码也不能解锁整个 App。忘记相册密码，照片将永久无法打开，没有任何找回办法。
  • 导入后自动删除原图：只有照片已经成功加密保存后，Tracejar 才会请求 iOS 从系统相册删除你本次选中的原图。iOS 会要求你确认；删除的原图会在「最近删除」中保留 30 天。若没有授予完整相册权限或删除失败，原图会保留，App 会明确提示。
  • 私密相册里每张照片的像素尺寸、加入时间以未加密形式和密文一起保存，用于排列网格；照片内容及其原始元数据（EXIF、GPS 等）都在密文里。
  • 私密相册的生物识别（Face ID 或 Touch ID，依设备而定）有独立开关。开启并输入过一次相册密码后，可以在这台设备上用当前生物识别打开相册：主钥匙被保存在本机 Keychain、受当前生物识别保护（WhenPasscodeSetThisDeviceOnly + biometryCurrentSet），不会同步；关闭开关会立即删除这份生物识别缓存。
  • 为防止同一 Apple ID 下的新设备装上 Tracejar 就能直接看日记，「已开启 App 锁」这一标记会经 iCloud 钥匙串同步；新设备第一次打开时需要输入 App 锁密码，而不是相册密码。
  • 伪装（Decoy）模式中显示的假记录，是你自己写的，只存在本机；运行时使用内存数据库，永不同步到 iCloud，关闭 App 即清空。
  • 该功能仅用于保护你设备上的本地隐私（例如防止他人随手翻看时看到敏感内容），不用于规避任何法律义务。

7. 照片元数据

从相册导入的照片/视频按原样保存，可能保留其原始元数据（如 EXIF、拍摄时间、GPS 坐标）。这些数据同样只留在你的设备与你的私人 iCloud 中，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

8. 导出

「导出 CSV」由你主动触发，文件在本机临时生成，通过系统分享面板由你自己决定发送到哪里。导出不包含照片/视频/语音的二进制内容（仅记数量）。

9. 你的权利

你可以随时在 App 内查看、编辑、删除任何记录；随时关闭 iCloud 同步；删除 App 即删除本机数据；在 iCloud 设置中可删除 iCloud 副本。由于我们不持有服务器端数据，这些操作全部由你直接完成，无需向我们申请。

10. 儿童

Tracejar 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其信息。

English

1. In one sentence

Tracejar has no servers. Your entries live only on your device and — if you turn on iCloud Sync — in your own private iCloud. The developer cannot access any of your content. We do not collect, analyze, or sell your data. There are no ads and no third-party SDKs.

2. Who we are

Tracejar is developed independently by Tongwei Zhang. Contact: zhangtongwei@gmail.com

3. What we do NOT collect

  • ❌ No phone number, email, contacts, or account (no sign-up required)
  • ❌ No device identifiers, no IDFA
  • ❌ No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads
  • ❌ No tracking across apps or websites

The app's bundled Privacy Manifest declares no collected data types.

4. Where your data lives

Your entries (text, photos, videos, voice notes, mood, tags, optional location) are stored:

1. Locally on your device (via Apple's SwiftData); and 2. In your own private iCloud database — only if you enable "iCloud Sync" in Settings (on by default). This is your Apple ID's private area; the developer has no access to it.

If you use a mainland-China Apple ID, iCloud is operated by GCBD (云上贵州). With Apple IDs from other regions, data is stored by Apple Inc. in the applicable jurisdiction. We hold no server-side data.

6. App Lock, Decoy Mode & Private Album (optional)

  • Your App Lock passcode is never stored or sent in readable form. Each device keeps a random salt + SHA-256 hash in its Keychain. Devices signed in to the same Apple ID may receive an encrypted verification envelope through iCloud Keychain; it contains only a random verification key, never journal content or the album key.
  • The Private Album has a separate passcode. Photos you add are encrypted on your device (AES-GCM, CryptoKit). Their random master key is locked with a key derived from the album passcode (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). Only the locked master-key envelope enters your private iCloud database and iCloud Keychain. The App Lock passcode cannot open the album, and the album passcode cannot unlock the whole app. If you forget the album passcode, the photos can never be opened again. There is no recovery.
  • Originals are removed after import: only after a photo has been encrypted and saved successfully does Tracejar ask iOS to remove the selected original from Photos. iOS asks you to confirm, and the original remains in Recently Deleted for 30 days. If full Photos access is unavailable or deletion fails, the original remains and the app explains why.
  • For each Private Album photo, its pixel dimensions and the time it was added are stored unencrypted alongside the encrypted data so the grid can be laid out. The photo itself and its original metadata (EXIF, GPS, etc.) are inside the encrypted data.
  • The Private Album has its own biometric switch, labeled Face ID or Touch ID according to the device. After you enter the album passcode once, you can open it with biometrics on that device when the switch is on: the unlocked key is kept in that device's Keychain, protected by the current biometric enrollment (WhenPasscodeSetThisDeviceOnly + biometryCurrentSet), and is never synced. Turning the switch off immediately removes that biometric copy.
  • So that a new device signed in to your Apple ID can't simply open your journal, an "App Lock is on" flag is synced through iCloud Keychain; a new device asks for your App Lock passcode the first time it opens.
  • The fake entries shown in Decoy Mode are written by you, kept on-device only, held in an in-memory database at runtime, and are never synced to iCloud.
  • This feature exists solely to protect your local privacy (e.g., so a person glancing at your phone doesn't see sensitive content). It is not intended to evade any legal obligation.

7. Photo metadata

Photos and videos imported from your library are stored as-is and may retain their original metadata (EXIF, capture time, GPS). That metadata likewise stays on your device and in your private iCloud, and is never sent to us or any third party.

8. Export

"Export as CSV" is user-initiated. The file is generated temporarily on-device and shared wherever you choose via the system share sheet. Photos/videos/audio binaries are not included (only counts).

9. Your rights

You can view, edit, and delete any entry at any time in the app; turn off iCloud Sync at any time; delete the app to remove local data; and delete the iCloud copy from iCloud settings. Because we hold no server-side data, you perform all of this directly — no request to us is needed.

10. Children

Tracejar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
